MyGov Submission Approval Evidence
Documentary Record

This repository preserves documentary evidence relating to a submission made by Kallol Chakrabarti, Independent Researcher, on the Government of India’s MyGov platform.

The submission was made in response to the MyGov discussion:

“Inviting Ideas for Mann Ki Baat by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on 30th August 2026.”

Evidence Included

This repository contains screenshots documenting:

The submission posted on the MyGov platform.
The MyGov platform displaying the submitted message.
The official SMS notification indicating that the submission was approved.
Purpose

The purpose of this repository is to preserve a clear, dated, and publicly referenceable record of the submission and the subsequent approval communication.

The materials are retained as documentary evidence for:

Personal academic and research records
Documentation of citizen participation
Preservation of the approval notification
Future reference and verification
